# Epic: Guest Travel Planning

## Business Role: Guest/Customer

## Business Objective

Enable guests to discover, plan, and book comprehensive travel experiences including accommodations, transportation, and various services offered by hosts on the platform.

## Business Value

- Provides guests with a comprehensive platform to plan their complete travel experience
- Simplifies the process of finding and booking multiple travel services in one marketplace
- Enhances travel experience through easy discovery of diverse host offerings
- Reduces planning time and complexity for travelers
- Creates a trusted marketplace similar to Fiverr but focused on travel services

## User Stories

### As a guest, I want to search for accommodations so that I can find suitable places to stay during my trip

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Guest can search for accommodations by location, dates, and preferences
- Search results show available properties with basic information, pricing, and host ratings
- Guest can filter search results by price, property type, amenities, and host ratings
- Search results distinguish between different types of hosts (individuals, businesses, etc.)

### As a guest, I want to view detailed information about accommodations so that I can make informed booking decisions

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Detailed property information includes photos, descriptions, amenities, and transparent pricing
- Guest can see real-time availability for specific dates
- Property location is displayed on a map with nearby attractions and services
- Host profile information, reviews, and ratings from other guests are visible
- Clear booking policies and cancellation terms are displayed

### As a guest, I want to discover and book experiences and activities so that I can enhance my travel experience

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Guest can browse available experiences offered by hosts in their destination
- Experience details include descriptions, duration, pricing, availability, and host information
- Booking process is integrated with the overall travel planning platform
- Guest can see host ratings and reviews for experiences
- Confirmation details and host contact information are provided after successful booking

### As a guest, I want to find transportation options so that I can plan how to get around

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Transportation services offered by hosts are displayed based on the guest's itinerary
- Information includes pricing, availability, booking details, and host ratings
- Different transportation types are available (car rental, tours, transfers, etc.)
- Integration with accommodation and experience locations for seamless planning

### As a guest, I want to create and manage my travel itinerary so that I can organize my trip details

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Guest can create a comprehensive trip itinerary with multiple components
- Itinerary includes accommodations, experiences, transportation, and other services
- Guest can view and modify their itinerary as needed
- Itinerary shows host information and contact details for each booking
- Itinerary can be shared or exported for reference

### As a guest, I want to communicate with hosts so that I can get clarifications and coordinate services

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Guest can send messages to hosts before, during, and after bookings
- Communication history is maintained for each host interaction
- Guest can request modifications or special arrangements through the messaging system
- Host response times and communication quality are reflected in ratings

## Business Rules

- All bookings must include guest contact information and verification
- Pricing must be transparent and include all applicable fees
- Cancellation policies must be clearly communicated before booking
- Guests must agree to terms and conditions before completing bookings
- Guest behavior and communication standards must be maintained

## Dependencies

- Host listings and services must be available and up-to-date
- Host availability and pricing must be maintained in real-time
- Payment processing system must be integrated and secure
- User authentication and verification system for managing bookings and profiles
- Messaging and communication system for guest-host interactions

## Success Metrics

- Number of successful bookings completed by guests
- Guest satisfaction with the booking and travel experience
- Reduction in time spent planning travel compared to traditional methods
- Increase in repeat bookings and guest retention
- Guest satisfaction with host interactions and service quality
