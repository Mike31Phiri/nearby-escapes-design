# Epic: Provider Experience Management

## Business Role: Experience Provider

## Business Objective

Enable experience providers and activity organizers to list, manage, and promote their tours, activities, and experiences to travelers, creating additional revenue opportunities and enhancing the local tourism ecosystem.

## Business Value

- Provides experience providers with a platform to showcase their unique offerings to travelers
- Creates additional revenue streams beyond traditional accommodations
- Enhances the overall travel experience by connecting travelers with authentic local experiences
- Supports local tourism businesses and cultural preservation
- Complements accommodation bookings with activity options

## User Stories

### As an experience provider, I want to register and create experience listings so that I can showcase my activities to travelers

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Provider can create an account with business and contact information
- Experience listing includes detailed descriptions, photos, and duration
- Provider can specify experience type (tour, activity, workshop, etc.)
- Location and meeting point information is captured
- Pricing per person or group can be set with different tiers

### As an experience provider, I want to manage my experience availability so that I can control when my activities can be booked

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Provider can set available dates and times for experiences
- Calendar view shows scheduled experiences and booking status
- Provider can set capacity limits for each experience session
- Minimum and maximum group sizes can be configured
- Seasonal availability and special schedules can be managed

### As an experience provider, I want to view and manage booking requests so that I can confirm reservations for my experiences

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Provider receives notifications for new experience bookings
- Booking details include participant information, date/time, and group size
- Provider can confirm or decline booking requests
- Communication channel with travelers is available
- Participant lists and special requirements can be managed

### As an experience provider, I want to manage my experience information so that I can keep my offerings current and attractive

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Provider can edit experience details, descriptions, and photos
- Inclusions, exclusions, and requirements can be updated
- Pricing and availability can be modified as needed
- Experience policies (cancellation, requirements, etc.) can be managed
- Changes are reflected immediately in the traveler-facing platform

### As an experience provider, I want to track my experience performance so that I can optimize my offerings and pricing

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Dashboard shows key metrics (booking rate, revenue, popular experiences)
- Provider can view traveler reviews and ratings for experiences
- Comparison with similar experiences in the area is available
- Reports on booking patterns and customer demographics can be generated
- Insights on peak demand periods and pricing optimization are provided

### As an experience provider, I want to manage my earnings and payments so that I can track revenue from experience bookings

**Acceptance Criteria:**

- Provider can view earnings from completed experiences
- Payment history and transaction details are accessible
- Payout schedules and methods can be configured
- Commission and fee information is clearly displayed
- Financial reports can be generated for business accounting

## Business Rules

- All experience listings must include accurate descriptions and pricing
- Providers must maintain current availability and capacity information
- Experience providers must have necessary licenses and permits
- Safety requirements and participant guidelines must be clearly communicated
- Providers must respond to booking requests within specified timeframes

## Dependencies

- User authentication and profile management system
- Payment processing and commission system
- Calendar and scheduling management system
- Notification and communication system
- Review and rating system
- Geographic location services for experience mapping

## Success Metrics

- Number of active experience listings on the platform
- Booking conversion rate for experiences
- Provider satisfaction with the experience management system
- Revenue generated for experience providers
- Average rating and reviews for experiences
- Integration rate with accommodation bookings
