
# Epic: Traveler Travel Planning

## Business Role: Traveler/Customer

## Business Objective
Enable travelers to discover, plan, and book travel experiences including accommodations, attractions, transportation, and activities in their desired destinations.

## Business Value
- Provides travelers with a comprehensive platform to plan their complete travel experience
- Simplifies the process of finding and booking multiple travel components in one place
- Enhances travel experience through easy discovery of local attractions and experiences
- Reduces planning time and complexity for travelers

## User Stories

### As a traveler, I want to search for accommodations so that I can find suitable places to stay during my trip
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Traveler can search for accommodations by location
- Search results show available properties with basic information
- Traveler can filter search results by price, property type, and amenities

### As a traveler, I want to view detailed information about accommodations so that I can make informed booking decisions
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Detailed property information includes photos, descriptions, amenities, and pricing
- Traveler can see availability for specific dates
- Property location is displayed on a map
- Reviews and ratings from other travelers are visible

### As a traveler, I want to discover nearby attractions so that I can plan activities during my stay
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- System shows attractions near the traveler's location or accommodation
- Attraction information includes descriptions, photos, and operating hours
- Traveler can filter attractions by type and interest
- Distance from accommodation is displayed for each attraction

### As a traveler, I want to find transportation options so that I can plan how to get around
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Transportation options are displayed based on the traveler's itinerary
- Information includes pricing, availability, and booking details
- Different transportation types are available (e.g., car rental, tours, transfers)
- Integration with accommodation and attraction locations

### As a traveler, I want to book experiences and activities so that I can enhance my travel experience
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Traveler can browse available experiences in their destination
- Experience details include descriptions, duration, pricing, and availability
- Booking process is integrated with the overall travel planning
- Confirmation details are provided after successful booking

### As a traveler, I want to create and manage my travel itinerary so that I can organize my trip details
**Acceptance Criteria:**
- Traveler can create a trip itinerary with multiple components
- Itinerary includes accommodations, attractions, transportation, and experiences
- Traveler can view and modify their itinerary as needed
- Itinerary can be shared or exported for reference

## Business Rules
- All bookings must include traveler contact information
- Pricing must be clearly displayed before booking confirmation
- Cancellation policies must be clearly communicated
- Traveler must agree to terms and conditions before completing bookings

## Dependencies
- Property listings must be available and up-to-date
- Attraction and experience providers must maintain current availability
- Payment processing system must be integrated
- User authentication system for managing bookings and profiles

## Success Metrics
- Number of successful travel bookings completed
- User satisfaction with travel planning experience
- Reduction in time spent planning travel compared to traditional methods
- Increase in bundled bookings (accommodation + attractions + experiences)
